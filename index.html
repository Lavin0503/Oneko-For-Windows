Oneko for Windows

A tiny pixel cat that chases your mouse around the screen. She never blocks a click, and she’s fully customizable.

Terminal Version

Turn it on and the cat pixelates whenever she wanders over a terminal window, then snaps back when she leaves. Choose how chunky she gets, or add a green phosphor tint.

~ desktop

normal cat

C:\> terminal

C:\> oneko_

Get her in 3 steps

Works on Windows 10 and 11. No installer needed.

  1. Download Oneko.exe from the latest GitHub release.
  2. Double-click it. If SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher, choose “More info” then “Run anyway”.
  3. Find the cat in the system tray (near the clock) to open settings, pause her or quit.
